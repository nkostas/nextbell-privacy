NextBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Summary: NextBell does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your schedules and app settings are stored locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how NextBell, developed by Nikolo Studio, handles information when you use the application.

Information we collect

NextBell does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone data, or other identifying information.

Local data storage

Schedules, preferences, theme choices, language settings, and other app settings are stored locally on your device. NextBell does not send this information to a Nikolo Studio server or cloud service.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

NextBell does not currently use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or tracking technologies.

Data sharing

NextBell does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties because the app does not collect personal data.

Internet and cloud services

The current version of NextBell does not require an account, cloud synchronization, or a Nikolo Studio backend service for its normal operation.

Data deletion

Because NextBell stores its data locally on your device, you can remove that data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

NextBell is a general-purpose schedule and time-management utility and is not specifically designed for children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if NextBell's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Nikolo Studio at nikolostudio@outlook.com.gr.
